Building new can be one of the most profitable moves in real estate, and one of the most capital-intensive. A ground-up construction loan funds the build in stages so you don't have to bring all the money yourself.

## What is a ground-up construction loan?

A short-term loan that funds building a new property from scratch: a single-family home, a townhome project or a small multifamily building. Like a fix and flip loan, it's usually interest only, and construction money is paid out in **draws** as the build progresses.

## What the loan typically covers

- **Land or lot:** part of the purchase price, or credit for land you already own
- **Hard costs:** materials and labor
- **Soft costs:** sometimes architect, engineering and permit fees, depending on the lender

## What lenders want to see

- **Plans and permits,** or a clear path to getting them
- **A detailed budget** from your builder
- **A realistic timeline** for construction and sale or lease-up
- **Builder experience,** since construction is more complex than a renovation
- **The completed value,** supported by comps for new builds nearby
- **Your exit:** selling, or refinancing into a long-term rental loan

## The biggest risks, and how to manage them

- **Delays:** permits, inspections, weather and labor shortages all add months. Build extra time into your plan.
- **Cost overruns:** lumber and materials prices move. Keep a healthy contingency.
- **Market changes:** a build takes longer than a flip, so the market may shift before you sell. Run your numbers conservatively.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do you need down for a construction loan as an investor?

It varies by lender and experience. Many construction lenders fund a large share of the build costs but expect the borrower to contribute equity, often through the land or a cash down payment.

### Can I get a construction loan without building experience?

It's harder, but possible if you work with an experienced general contractor or builder and have solid plans, a realistic budget and enough reserves.

### How do draws work on a construction loan?

Construction funds are released in stages after inspections confirm each phase of work is complete, similar to rehab draws on a fix and flip. Read [how rehab draws work](/blog/how-rehab-draws-work) for the details.
