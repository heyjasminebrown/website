If you're building a rental portfolio, there's a good chance you'll hit a wall with traditional mortgages: too many properties, self-employment income that's hard to document, or a debt-to-income ratio that doesn't fit the box. A DSCR loan is built for exactly this.

## What does DSCR stand for?

DSCR stands for **debt service coverage ratio**. Instead of qualifying you based on your personal income, a DSCR loan qualifies the **property** based on whether its rent covers its payment.

## How to calculate DSCR

DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly payment

The monthly payment usually includes principal, interest, property taxes, insurance and any HOA dues.

**Example:** a property rents for $2,200 a month, and the full monthly payment is $1,850.

$2,200 ÷ $1,850 = **1.19 DSCR**

A ratio above 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Many lenders look for a DSCR of around 1.0 to 1.25, though requirements vary.

## Why investors use DSCR loans

- **No personal income verification** in the traditional sense, which helps self-employed investors
- **Scales with your portfolio,** since each property qualifies on its own numbers
- **Can close in an LLC,** keeping your rentals separate from your personal finances
- **Works for the refinance step** after a renovation, sometimes called the BRRRR strategy (buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat)

## What lenders look at

- The property's rent (from a lease or a market rent estimate)
- The DSCR
- Your credit score
- The loan-to-value ratio (how much you're borrowing compared with what the property is worth)
- Cash reserves after closing

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good DSCR for a rental property?

A DSCR of 1.25 or higher is generally considered strong, because the rent covers the payment with room to spare. Many lenders will still consider a DSCR around 1.0, and some programs go lower with a larger down payment.

### Do DSCR loans require tax returns?

DSCR loans typically qualify you based on the property's rental income rather than your personal tax returns, which is why they're popular with self-employed investors and people with several properties.

### Can I use a DSCR loan to refinance after a flip or rehab?

Yes. Many investors buy and renovate with a short-term loan, rent the property out, then refinance into a DSCR loan to pay off the short-term loan and hold the rental long term.

### Can I get a DSCR loan in an LLC?

Yes, most DSCR loans can close in an LLC or other business entity, which many investors prefer for their rentals.
