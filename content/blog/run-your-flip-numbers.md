The profit in a flip is made when you buy, not when you sell. Here's how to check a deal in about five minutes before you make an offer.

## 1. Start with three numbers

- **Purchase price:** what you'll pay.
- **Rehab budget:** everything it takes to get the property market-ready, including staging and permits.
- **ARV (after repair value):** what it should sell for when it's done, based on recent sold comps nearby.

## 2. Add your financing

Enter how much of the purchase and rehab your lender will fund, the interest rate, the points and how long you expect to hold the property. Be honest about hold time: rehab, time on the market and closing usually take longer than you hope.

## 3. Don't forget carrying, buying and selling costs

These are where flips quietly lose money:

- **Carrying costs:** your interest-only payment, HOA and utilities, every month you own it.
- **Acquisition costs:** closing costs, property taxes and insurance when you buy.
- **Selling costs:** realtor fees and closing costs when you sell. Together these are often 4% or more of the sale price.

## 4. Look at net profit and ROI

If the profit doesn't leave room for surprises, like a longer hold or a rehab that runs over, either renegotiate the price or walk away. Try changing the hold time or the rehab budget to see how sensitive your profit is.
